Fig. S1. Retrospective two-map benchmarking of ROI placement at the same number of ROIs (N = 12 per map). (a) Conditional finite-design coefficient of variation (CV) and (b) root-mean-square error (RMSE) relative to the full-map image-derived hotspot proxy for map c (laminated shale) and map g (laminated shell-bearing shale). Simple random ROI positions are compared with the frozen stratified sampling design under identical ROI geometry and valid-pixel rules. The dashed line in (a) marks the prespecified CV ceiling of 0.20. The calculations describe positional sampling of an image proxy and do not represent independently repeated confocal measurements.
